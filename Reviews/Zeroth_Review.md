## 1. Motivation
The traditional financial landscape is heavily centralized, creating single points of failure, lack of transparency, and high overhead costs. While decentralized finance (DeFi) offers a solution, many existing platforms are overly complex for average users. Our motivation is to bridge the gap between traditional banking usability and the cryptographic security of Web3. By building a decentralized banking system, we aim to empower users with full control over their assets while maintaining a familiar interface.

## 2. Objectives
- **Decentralized Control:** Eliminate the need for intermediaries in standard banking operations.
- **Enhanced Security:** Implement multi-signature approvals for high-value transactions.
- **Improved UX:** Create a modern, intuitive dashboard for blockchain interactions.
- **Feature Richness:** Support deposits, P2P payments, automated recurring payments, and real-time gas estimation.
- **Transparency:** Maintain an immutable ledger on the Ethereum Sepolia Testnet.

## 3. Problem Survey
Existing banking systems and DeFi DApps face several challenges:
- **Centralized Security Risks:** Traditional banks are vulnerable to systemic failures and censorship.
- **Complexity in DeFi:** High barrier to entry for non-technical users in current blockchain payment systems.
- **Gas Fee Uncertainty:** Lack of real-time estimation tools makes transactions unpredictable for users.
- **Lack of Advanced Controls:** Traditional crypto-wallets often lack simple features like daily spending limits or scheduled payments.

## 4. Subject Knowledge
To successfully implement this project, the team has acquired proficiency in the following:
- **Smart Contract Development:** Solidity (^0.8.20), Hardhat, and Remix IDE.
- **Web3 Integration:** Ethers.js v6 for blockchain-to-frontend communication.
- **Frontend Architecture:** React 18, Vite, and modern CSS frameworks.
- **Security Patterns:** Pull Payment pattern, Reentrancy Guards, and Multi-signature logic.
- **Blockchain Infrastructure:** Ethereum Sepolia Testnet nodes and RPC providers.
